Ad Interceptor is on

Ads are already being blocked.

There is nothing to set up. Three things are worth knowing.

1

Pin it so you can see it working

Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin beside Ad Interceptor. The number on the icon is how many ad and tracker requests it stopped on the page you are looking at.

2

If a site misbehaves, pause it there

Open the Ad Interceptor button and press Pause. Blocking stops on that site only, and everywhere else stays protected. Press it again to resume.

3

What the free version does not cover

Ads built into a video player — YouTube pre-rolls, for example — and "disable your ad blocker" walls need Ad Interceptor Pro. Everything else is blocked for free, permanently.
