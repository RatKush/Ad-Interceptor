What it does

  • Blocks ad and tracker requests before they leave your browser. The ad never downloads, so pages load faster and lighter — this is not cosmetic hiding of something already fetched.
  • Removes the empty gaps left behind. {{cosmeticCount}} element-hiding rules mean a blocked page looks like a page, not a page full of holes.
  • Handles pop-ups and pop-unders as thoroughly as banner ads, including on the parts of the web that push them hardest.
  • Pause on one site with a single tap, without changing anything anywhere else. Some sites genuinely break under aggressive blocking; this is the escape hatch.
  • Shows you the count — how many requests were blocked on this page, and how many since you installed it.

How the blocking works

Rules are compiled from EasyList and EasyPrivacy, the community-maintained filter lists that serious ad blockers have relied on for years. They ship inside the extension package and are evaluated by Chrome itself, on your machine, using the browser's own declarativeNetRequest engine.

No request is ever sent to us to decide what to block, because there is no "us" in the loop. The free version makes no network requests at all.
