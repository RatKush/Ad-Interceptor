What Pro actually adds

In-stream video ads

Advertising entries are removed from the player's response on major video platforms, so the ad is never requested — rather than being hidden after it has already loaded.

Anti-adblock wall bypass

The "please disable your ad blocker" overlays. Powered by EasyList's dedicated Adblock Warning Removal List, updated daily.

Daily filter updates

EasyList and EasyPrivacy change every day, but rules bundled inside an extension can only be refreshed by shipping a new version through the Chrome Web Store. Pro fetches a freshly built rule set directly, so new rules apply the day they are published. This is what the subscription funds.

Why the free version is not crippled

Free keeps the complete filter set permanently. It is not a trial, it does not expire, and no rules are withheld from it to make Pro look better. Pro exists to fund the maintenance, not to ransom the basics.
