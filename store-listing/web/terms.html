2. What the extension is

Ad Interceptor is a browser extension that blocks advertising and tracking requests, and hides leftover advertising containers on web pages. Blocking rules are compiled from the publicly available EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists and are evaluated by your browser on your own device.

The free version is provided at no cost and includes the complete filter set. Pro is an optional paid subscription adding in-stream video ad removal on major video platforms, anti-adblock overlay removal, and a daily-refreshed filter feed.
