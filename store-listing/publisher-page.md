# Publisher page description

The Chrome Web Store dashboard's "Public publisher page" is a separate,
per-account profile shown across all your extensions — distinct from
description.md, which is the per-item listing for Ad Interceptor alone.
Paste one of these into that field (there's a short character limit;
the short version is the safe default).

---

## Short (safe default)

> Building small, focused browser tools that respect your data — both
> the bytes you download and the information you never had to hand
> over. Ad Interceptor is the first: block every ad, on every site,
> with no analytics and nothing collected along the way.

## Shorter still, if the field truncates

> Small browser tools that do one thing well, and collect nothing
> along the way. Ad Interceptor is the first.

description should stop being product-specific and describe your
publisher identity generally — swap it out at that point.*
