! Title: Ad Interceptor — supplemental rules
! Description: Our own rules, for ads the upstream lists miss or under-block.
! Syntax: standard Adblock Plus filter syntax. Converted by
!   scripts/build-filters.mjs into rules/custom-1.json, which is listed FIRST
!   in the manifest and always enabled — so a fix here can never be the chunk
!   that gets dropped when static-rule quota runs short.
!
! Use $important on domain blocks that upstream deliberately under-blocks.
! It outranks the exception rules in EasyList, but still sits below the
! per-site pause and master-off overrides (priority 2,000,000), so the user
! can always overrule us.
!
! Adding a rule: state the date, the report, and WHY upstream didn't cover it.
! A rule without that context is one nobody can safely remove later.

! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! 2026-08-29 — html-load.com ad-proxy family
!
! Reported live on www.catforum.com (6.stg / 9.stg subdomains) and on
! cookieandkate.com (bare 8. subdomain). These hosts proxy ad content
! behind randomised subdomains and paths that embed the visited site's own
! hostname (/session/<rand>/<rand>/www.catforum.com/...), specifically so the
! request looks first-party and defeats path- and domain-pattern filters.
! Confirmed serving image/png ad creatives via Cloudflare.
!
! EasyList lists this family but blocks it ONLY for popups
! (`||html-load.com^$popup`, `||html-load.com^$document,popup`), which
! converts to a main_frame-only DNR rule — so ads loaded as images, scripts
! or sub-frames were never blocked. Only the .cc sibling is blocked outright
! upstream. These rules close that gap across all resource types.
!
! Sibling domains taken from EasyList's own grouping of this family
! (easylist.txt: `$popup,domain=07c225f3.online|content-loader.com|
! css-load.com|html-load.cc|html-load.com|img-load.com`).
!
! Subdomains rotate freely (6.stg, 9.stg, 8, ...), which is why these are
! ||domain^ rules rather than anything host-specific.
!
! TRADE-OFF: this is a proxy service, so a site could in principle route real
! assets through it. Blocking the whole domain is the aggressive choice and
! matches this product's stance; per-site Pause is the escape hatch if a site
! turns out to depend on it.
||html-load.com^$important
||html-load.cc^$important
||content-loader.com^$important
||css-load.com^$important
||img-load.com^$important
||07c225f3.online^$important
